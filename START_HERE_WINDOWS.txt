HOW TO START COMFYUI ON WINDOWS
===============================

You do not need to know how to code. You only double-click files.
(On Linux: run  ./start_comfyui.sh  and  ./settings_api_keys.sh  instead. Everything else is the same.)


1. GET THE FOLDER
   If you received a link to GitHub: click the green "Code" button, then "Download ZIP".
   Right-click the ZIP, choose "Extract All", and put the folder somewhere simple,
   for example C:\ComfyUI  (a short path without special characters works best).


2. START COMFYUI
   Open the folder and double-click  START_COMFYUI.bat

   - If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and then "Run anyway".
   - If Python is not installed, the black window asks you to install it. Press Y.
     If that does not work, install Python 3.13 from https://www.python.org/downloads/windows/
     (tick "Add python.exe to PATH" at the bottom of the first installer screen),
     then double-click START_COMFYUI.bat again.
   - The FIRST time, it downloads a few GB and can take 10-30 minutes. Let it finish.
   - A small window for API keys opens once. Fill in what you were given, or just click Save.

   When it is ready, ComfyUI opens in your web browser by itself.
   If it does not, open the browser and go to  http://127.0.0.1:8188

   Keep the black window open while you work. Closing it turns ComfyUI off.
   Next time, just double-click START_COMFYUI.bat again: it starts in under a minute.


3. PICK A WORKFLOW: WHAT IS FREE, WHAT COSTS MONEY
   In ComfyUI click  Workflow -> Open  and pick a file from the "ai_influencer" folder.
   Then click "Run". Your pictures are saved in the "output" folder.

   There are two kinds of workflows:

   ONLINE (costs Comfy credits, works on any computer, no downloads)
     01  Character creation (makes 16 big pictures per full run: this adds up)
     04  Photo to video
     05  Automated production
     06  Train your own character model (uses fal.ai credits instead, with its own key)
     These use paid online services. Each paid box shows its price before you click Run.
     The credits come from the Comfy account you signed in with.

   ON YOUR OWN GRAPHICS CARD (free to run, but needs a strong card and big downloads)
     07, 08, 09, 09b, 10   Make pictures with Krea 2 on your computer
     These need AI model files (about 14-23 GB, downloaded once and shared by all of them)
     and an NVIDIA card with 8 GB of memory or more (RTX 3060 Ti or better). On a weaker card
     or a laptop, use the online workflows instead.
     11  Video with MiniMax: only on a rented pod (needs a huge graphics card)

   FREE AND LIGHT
     09c  Practice version of 09b, to learn the buttons (no downloads, no graphics card)
     12   Adds a camera look (grain, colour) to a picture you already have
     13   Pick the best pictures from a batch


4. "MISSING MODELS": DO I HAVE TO DOWNLOAD THEM?
   When you open a workflow from the "own graphics card" group, ComfyUI lists the model files
   it uses on the right side. You do NOT have to download anything just to look at a workflow.
   Ignore that list. Only when you click "Run" does a window open that:
     - tells you if your graphics card can run it at all (if not, it says so: don't download),
     - explains each file and its size,
     - lets you pick the Krea 2 version that fits your card:
         Low memory (GGUF)    8-11 GB cards: RTX 3060 Ti, 3070, 4060, 5060
                              (also installs a free add-on; restart ComfyUI once afterwards)
         Standard (FP8)       cards with 12 GB or more
         Small (NVFP4)        RTX 50-series cards with 12 GB or more
         Full quality (BF16)  very big cards only (40 GB or more)
       The window marks the one that fits your card as "recommended".
     - and downloads only the files that workflow needs, into the right folder.
   You can open that window any time: Extensions menu -> "AI Influencer: Models".


5. CHANGE YOUR API KEYS LATER
   Double-click  SETTINGS_API_KEYS.bat , fill in the boxes, click Save,
   then close the black ComfyUI window and start START_COMFYUI.bat again.
   Each key has a "Get key" button that opens the website where you create it.
   Keys cost money when used. Never send your keys (or the .env file) to anyone.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
   - Read the last lines in the black window; they usually say what happened.
   - Without an NVIDIA graphics card, ComfyUI runs on the processor. The online workflows
     still work; the "own graphics card" ones are far too slow there.
   - To redo the installation, delete the "venv" folder and double-click START_COMFYUI.bat.
   - Still stuck? Take a screenshot of the black window and send it to the person who
     shared this folder with you.
